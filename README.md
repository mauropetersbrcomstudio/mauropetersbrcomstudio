## How I approach AI systems

I favour clear contracts over opaque autonomy: explicit inputs and outputs, bounded permissions, human escalation for ambiguity, reproducible tests, and honest documentation of what a system does not do.

Demonstrations are labelled as demonstrations. Synthetic data is identified as synthetic. A passing test is evidence only for the behaviour covered by that test; it is not a claim of production readiness or business impact.
